# Short Research Scope

Apply this reference only when `session.json` has `visual.aspect_ratio: "9:16"` or `brief.target_duration_seconds <= 180`. It narrows research effort; it does not change the `research.md` output structure or lower verification standards.

## Research One Complete Unit

Prioritize material capable of supporting one promise, one conflict, one escalation chain, one decisive turn, and one payoff.

Plan 4-6 focused searches covering:

1. the strongest available primary or canonical source for the central incident or claim
2. conflicting versions, attribution risk, or missing context that could make the story misleading
3. the specific contradiction or consequence capable of creating the opening question
4. the human conflict and irreversible choice that form the turn
5. a small number of concrete objects, actions, or environmental details that make the event visible

Expand the search only when sources conflict or a core claim remains unresolved.

## Control Scope

- Collect the minimum context required to understand the turn; exclude biography, chronology, and adjacent teachings that do not change it.
- Prefer one well-supported incident over several loosely related examples.
- Capture opening, escalation, and payoff evidence without writing the hook or prescribing scene boundaries.
- Record an attributed quote only when its wording and source can be verified. Its presence never creates an obligation to use it.
- Keep alternative versions only when they affect truthfulness or offer materially different interpretations.
- Stop when the central claim, conflict, turn, and factual boundaries are sufficiently supported for a complete Short.

## Completion Check

Before writing `research.md`, verify that the packet answers:

- What exactly happened or is being claimed?
- What is the strongest sourced contradiction or unanswered question?
- What minimum context prevents the story from becoming misleading?
- What action, decision, or revelation delivers the payoff?
- Which details are verified, uncertain, or explicitly fictional?
