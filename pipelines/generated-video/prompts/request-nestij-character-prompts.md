# Request: Nestij Character-Sheet Prompts

You are a senior Indian advertising casting director and photorealistic image-prompt designer. Help define the two human characters for a serious Nestij advertisement aimed at Indian property and PG owners.

## Advertisement context

The story opens inside a clean but vacant rental room. Its owner is frustrated because the room has remained empty despite being listed elsewhere. A longtime friend notices the problem. The owner then lists the property on Nestij, and the story later returns to him receiving a direct tenant enquiry.

The owner is the emotional centre of the advertisement. The friend is a supporting presence and must not visually compete with him.

## Casting problem to solve

The owner must immediately feel like a believable Indian **PG owner/operator**—someone who personally manages rooms, tenants, maintenance, payments, and enquiries. He must not look like a generic corporate employee, polished bank manager, model, caricature, or comic landlord. Find an authentic visual identity through age, build, face, grooming, clothing, footwear, posture, and small practical details. Keep the portrayal respectful and recognisable without relying on crude stereotypes.

The friend should look like a natural peer from the owner's real social world while remaining clearly distinct in face, silhouette, colour palette, grooming, and wardrobe.

## Required output

Develop the characters independently:

1. **OWNER — Indian PG owner/operator**
2. **FRIEND — familiar peer and supporting character**

For each character, provide:

- a concise casting rationale explaining why the person fits the advertisement;
- a compact list of stable visual identity traits;
- one final, self-contained image-generation prompt for Google Flow's highest-quality photorealistic image model.

Each final prompt must request a clean portrait-format character reference sheet showing the same person consistently across:

- full-body front view;
- full-body three-quarter view;
- chest-up neutral portrait.

Use neutral studio lighting and a simple light-grey background. Require natural Indian facial identity, realistic anatomy and hands, complete visible footwear, restrained commercial realism, and no environment, furniture, extra people, labels, typography, logos, or watermark. Wardrobe and fixed accessories must remain identical across all views.

The two final prompts must stand alone: do not refer to “the description above,” the other prompt, or an unseen reference image. Make concrete casting decisions instead of returning broad alternatives. Do not generate images.

Present the final prompts in separate fenced blocks labelled `OWNER FLOW PROMPT` and `FRIEND FLOW PROMPT` so they can be copied without editing.
