# Part 1 recurring-character reference prompts

These are still-image prompts for reusable reference images. Every vegetable character has a natural human body; only the head and face carry the vegetable identity. Choose one coherent direction across the cast.

## Aloo — `@aloo`

### Option A — warm village hero

```text
Create a full-body reference portrait of Aloo, a brave village father for a polished high-energy children's 3D animated adventure. He has a natural adult human male body with realistic human proportions, human arms, hands, legs and feet. His head is a broad oval golden-brown potato with shallow natural dimples; large warm dark-brown eyes, strong expressive eyebrows, a friendly nose and mouth are naturally formed on the potato face. His fixed outfit is a teal cotton kurta, off-white pajama trousers, a bright red neck scarf and simple brown village shoes. He stands alert and protective, looking caring, dependable and physically capable. Tactile stylised 3D animation, vivid child-friendly colours, clear three-quarter front view, entire body visible, neutral warm-grey studio background, soft even reference lighting. No text, no scenery, no other characters, no vegetable-shaped torso, no extra limbs, no photorealistic human head.
```

### Option B — eccentric action hero

```text
Design Aloo as a funny but courageous village father for a whimsical high-retention 3D children's rescue cartoon. He has a natural sturdy adult human male body with normal shoulders, torso, arms, expressive human hands, legs and feet. Only his head is vegetable-like: an irregular chunky russet-potato head with memorable dimples, huge warm hazel eyes, one raised eyebrow, a small curled dark moustache and a highly expressive mouth. He wears a mustard-yellow kurta, loose cream pajama trousers, a cobalt-blue sleeveless waistcoat, a red cloth belt and rugged green village shoes. Use an energetic forward-leaning stance that reads as comic at first glance but unmistakably brave. Crisp full-body reference image, three-quarter front angle, bold silhouette, saturated cinematic animation colours, subtle clay-like potato texture only on the head, uncluttered pale-blue studio background, uniform lighting. No words, no props, no environment, no potato body, no additional figures.
```
