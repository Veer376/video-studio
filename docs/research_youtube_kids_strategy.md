## 3. Episodic Story Pacing (2–3 Minute Narrative Engine)
- **4-Beat Structure:**
  1. `0:00 – 0:20 (The Spark & Kinetic Hook)`: In media res opening (falling, flying, racing), high stakes introduced in first 3s with zero dialogue lag.
  2. `0:20 – 1:00 (Comic Escalation)`: First fix attempts backfire into funny physical slapstick (mud splash, runaway wheels).
  3. `1:00 – 2:00 (Peak Peril & Dragon Power-Up)`: Highest stakes (cliffhanger, villain challenge) solved by Angaar the baby dragon boosting the tractor.
  4. `2:00 – 2:30 (Slapstick Resolution & Button)`: Comedic defeat of antagonist, celebratory dance/samosas, 5s closing joke.
- **Mathematical Metrics:** Average shot length of 2.0–2.8 seconds, camera angle reset every 3 seconds, 1 Foley cue (boing, whoosh, tyre screech) every 2.5s.
- **12-Second Tension-Release Cycle:** 9 seconds of dramatic build broken by 3 seconds of physical comedy slapstick.
