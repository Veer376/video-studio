# Character and Style Continuity Guidelines

Maintaining character continuity and visual style across all scenes is critical to producing a high-quality storytelling video. This guide defines how to write image prompts that keep character features, clothing, and environment style consistent without visual jarring between cuts.

---

## 1. Core Visual Style & Art Direction

- **Style**: Premium cinematic digital painting.
- **Environment**: Ancient rural India (mud-brick houses, banyan trees, dirt paths, riverbanks).
- **Color Palette**: 
  - Warm Ochre (golden hour, evening light)
  - Saffron (rusts, oranges, sunset hues)
  - Muted Teal (shadows, cool backdrops)
  - Peacock Blue (drapery, sky, divine elements)
  - Warm Gold (ornaments, highlighted details)
- **Render Restrictions**:
  - NO modern objects (e.g., plastic, metallic glasses, modern stitching).
  - NO text, logos, or watermarks inside generated images.
  - NO kitschy devotional-poster treatment or cheap glowing halos.
  - NO neon aura or unnecessary sci-fi supernatural effects.
  - Realistic anatomy and proportions.

---

## 2. Character Descriptions (The Character Bible)

When writing prompts, reference the exact recurring features and garments of each character as listed below:

### Nandan (Protagonist)
- **Age/Physique**: Approximately 24 years old, warm brown skin, thoughtful expression.
- **Hair**: Shoulder-length dark wavy hair.
- **Attire**: Off-white cotton dhoti, muted rust angavastram (shoulder drape), simple leather sandals.
- **Vibe**: Earnest, seeking, learning, and humble.

### Krishna (Guide)
- **Age/Physique**: Youthful Indian man with serene, deep blue skin (not a sky-blue caricature, but a rich midnight blue), calm smile.
- **Hair**: Long dark wavy hair flowing naturally.
- **Headpiece**: One peacock feather tucked in his hair (no massive theatrical crowns).
- **Attire**: Understated golden-yellow pitambara (dhoti/silk waist cloth), simple gold wristbands, simple ornaments.
- **Accents**: Holding or wearing a bamboo flute.
- **Vibe**: Calm, warm, friendly, divine yet humble and accessible.

---

## 3. How to Write Image Prompts

For each scene, construct prompts that combine:
1. **Scene Composition**: Who is in the frame, what they are doing, and camera placement (e.g., "Krishna and Nandan watching a farmer...").
2. **Character Identifiers**: Explicitly include descriptions from the bible (e.g., "Nandan in off-white dhoti and rust angavastram, Krishna with deep blue skin and a single peacock feather in his dark hair").
3. **Environment and Style**: Set the scene and lighting (e.g., "ancient riverbank India, sunset lighting, premium cinematic digital painting").
4. **Style Negative Prompts/Constraints**: Reinforce what to avoid (e.g., "no text, no watermark, no neon halos").
